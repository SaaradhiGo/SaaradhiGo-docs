## 1. Nature of this relationship — IMPORTANT

1.1 You are an **independent service provider** offering transportation
services to riders. SaaradhiGo is a **technology aggregator** that
facilitates connections between riders and Driver Partners through its
Platform.

1.2 This Agreement is on a **principal-to-principal basis**. Nothing in this
Agreement creates an employer-employee, partnership, joint venture, agency,
or franchise relationship between SaaradhiGo and You.

1.3 You retain full control over the operation of Your vehicle, including:

- Whether and when to log into the Platform and accept requests
- Which routes to take (subject to road safety and traffic law)
- Whether to accept or reject any particular ride request (subject to the
  acceptance-rate expectations in Schedule B)
- Whether to operate on SaaradhiGo, on other platforms, or independently
- All operating costs of Your vehicle, fuel, maintenance, insurance, and
  taxes

1.4 You acknowledge that:

- You are not entitled to employee benefits (provident fund, gratuity, paid
  leave, ESI, etc.) from SaaradhiGo
- SaaradhiGo will not deduct income tax at source as if You were an employee;
  it will, however, deduct TDS under Section 194O of the Income Tax Act on
  Your gross fare collections through the Platform, as required by law
  (Section 9)
- You are responsible for filing Your own income tax returns and GST returns
  (if and when GST registration is applicable to You)
- You will obtain Your own health, accident, or disability insurance; the
  trip-specific insurance offered through the Platform (if any) does not
  replace personal insurance

## 3. Your obligations

### 3.1 Operating the vehicle

(a) Drive safely, lawfully, and in accordance with the Motor Vehicles Act,
the Central Motor Vehicle Rules, the Telangana Motor Vehicle Rules, and all
applicable traffic regulations.

(b) Maintain Your vehicle in good operating condition, clean inside and
out, with working AC (for AC-category vehicles), working seat belts, valid
fire extinguisher and first-aid box (as required by the State Transport
Department).

(c) Display vehicle ID and permit information as required by Telangana
state law and by SaaradhiGo's policies.

(d) Carry a printed or digital copy of valid documents during every trip.

(e) Do not refuse legitimate trips on the basis of route, distance, fare
mode (cash/online), or any protected characteristic. Acceptance rate
expectations are described in Schedule B.

(f) Do not operate the Platform while under the influence of alcohol, any
controlled substance, or while fatigued. Compliance with the 12-hour
operating-window cap defined in MVA 2020 is mandatory.

(g) Use the in-app SOS / panic button responsibly. Misuse is grounds for
deactivation.

### 3.2 Trip conduct

(a) Verify the rider's identity using the trip OTP shared by the rider
before starting the trip. Do not start the trip if the OTP does not match
the OTP displayed in the driver app.

(b) Take the route shown in the driver app or a reasonable alternative
based on traffic conditions. Avoid detours that materially extend the trip
without rider consent.

(c) Treat the rider, co-passengers, and other road users with respect at
all times. Verbal abuse, threats, or unwanted physical contact are grounds
for permanent deactivation and may result in legal action.

(d) Do not solicit off-platform trips, share Your personal contact details
with riders for off-platform engagement, or accept additional payment for
trips routed through the Platform.

(e) Do not photograph or record the rider except as part of the in-app
safety features (the trip OTP screen, accident photo upload flow, etc.).

(f) End the trip in the app only when the rider has reached the
destination.

### 3.3 Documents and data

(a) Keep all KYC documents current in the driver app. Upload renewed copies
at least 30 days before expiry.

(b) Do not falsify documents, identity, or vehicle details. Falsification
is grounds for permanent deactivation and criminal complaint.

(c) Maintain the confidentiality of any rider personal data shared with
You during a trip (name, phone, route). Sharing this data outside the trip
context, except as required by law or emergency, is a material breach.

### 3.4 Account hygiene

(a) Do not share Your driver app login with any other person. Each
Driver Partner is one specific individual; vehicle-sharing between
licensed drivers requires each driver to have their own SaaradhiGo
account.

(b) Use only the device registered to Your account.

(c) Notify SaaradhiGo immediately at {{SUPPORT_PHONE}} if Your account or
device is compromised.

## 4. SaaradhiGo's obligations

### 4.1 Platform availability

SaaradhiGo will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform
available 24x7 subject to scheduled maintenance and force majeure events.

### 4.2 Fair matching

The Platform's matching algorithm assigns ride requests based on proximity,
vehicle type, driver rating, acceptance rate, and other operational
factors. SaaradhiGo will operate the algorithm in a non-discriminatory
manner.

### 4.3 Fare collection and settlement

(a) Where the rider pays online, SaaradhiGo collects the fare on Your
behalf via its payment gateway partner and remits it to You (after
deductions per Section 6) on the payout schedule in Section 7.

(b) Where the rider pays in cash, the rider pays You directly; SaaradhiGo
does not handle the cash. SaaradhiGo will however charge You its commission
for the trip (Section 6).

### 4.4 Support

SaaradhiGo provides driver support through the driver app and at
{{SUPPORT_PHONE}}.

### 4.5 SOS response

SaaradhiGo's 24x7 control room responds to SOS events as described in the
SOS section below.

### 4.6 Data and privacy

SaaradhiGo processes Your personal data per the Privacy Policy.

## 5. Vehicle and trip insurance

5.1 You confirm that You hold and maintain (a) statutory third-party
liability insurance, and (b) comprehensive insurance with passenger cover
for the vehicle, both with a licensed Indian insurer.

5.2 In the event of an accident or claim:

(a) Stop the vehicle at a safe place; render first aid; notify SaaradhiGo
immediately via the in-app SOS or {{SUPPORT_PHONE}}; notify the police
(100) and ambulance (108) as appropriate.

(b) Cooperate fully with the insurer's claim process.

(c) SaaradhiGo will assist with documentation and witness statements where
the trip was on-platform but is not a party to Your insurance contract.

5.3 Where SaaradhiGo offers in-trip rider insurance through a partner, the
coverage applies to the rider and is governed by the partner's policy. It
does not affect Your insurance obligations.

## 6. Fees, commissions, and settlement

### 6.1 SaaradhiGo Service Fee

SaaradhiGo charges a **service fee** ("**Commission**") on the gross fare of
each trip routed through the Platform. The current commission rate is set
out in Schedule B and may be revised by SaaradhiGo with 30 days' notice.

### 6.2 Other charges

In addition to the Commission, the following may apply on Your settlement:

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Payment gateway fee pass-through | Where the rider pays online, the gateway's transaction fee may be deducted (currently absorbed by SaaradhiGo for Phase-0) |
| TDS u/s 194O | 1% statutory deduction on gross fare; see Section 9 |
| Promotional rebates | If a trip was discounted to the rider under a promotion, SaaradhiGo bears the discount (You receive the full quoted driver fare) |
| Adjustments | Trip cancellations, disputes, chargebacks |

### 6.3 Settlement currency

All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR).

## 7. Payouts

7.1 **Payout cadence.** SaaradhiGo pays out Your accrued, settled earnings
on the schedule defined in Schedule B (default: weekly, every Monday for
the preceding week).

7.2 **Method.** Payouts are made by UPI to the VPA on Your file (preferred)
or by IMPS/NEFT to the bank account on Your file.

7.3 **On-demand withdrawals.** You may request an on-demand withdrawal up
to a maximum of the available balance and a daily cap defined in Schedule
B. On-demand requests are typically processed within 2 business hours.

7.4 **Hold for risk / disputes.** SaaradhiGo may temporarily hold a
withdrawal amount where (a) a dispute with the rider is open, (b) the
gateway has flagged the transaction for review, (c) a chargeback is in
process, or (d) there is a reasonable suspicion of fraud. SaaradhiGo will
release or adjust held amounts within 30 days of resolution.

7.5 **Failed payouts.** If a payout fails because of incorrect bank/UPI
details on file, You will be notified and the amount returns to Your
available balance. Repeated failures may delay subsequent payouts until
You update Your details.

## 8. Ratings, deactivation, and termination

### 8.1 Ratings

After every completed trip, the rider may rate You 1–5 stars and leave a
text comment. Your average rating is visible to riders and to You in the
driver app.

### 8.2 Performance expectations

The driver app displays a "Driver Score" derived from rating, acceptance
rate, cancellation rate, on-time performance, and customer complaints.
Thresholds for warnings, time-outs, and de-activation are in Schedule B.

### 8.3 Deactivation

SaaradhiGo may suspend or de-activate Your account temporarily or permanently
for, including but not limited to:

- Document expiry or invalidation
- Violation of this Agreement
- Behaviour that endangers rider safety
- Verified complaint of harassment, abuse, or assault
- Fraud or attempted fraud
- Repeated cancellations or refusals
- Operating under the influence
- Conviction in any criminal proceeding affecting fitness to drive

Where the deactivation is for behavioural reasons, You will be given an
opportunity to respond before a final decision, except where immediate
suspension is required for rider safety. Appeal process is in Section 12.

### 8.4 Your right to terminate

You may stop using the Platform at any time. You may close Your account by
writing to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Outstanding settled amounts will be paid out
in the next regular cycle.

### 8.5 Effect of termination

On termination:

- Your driver app access is revoked
- Any in-progress trip is completed under SaaradhiGo's direction
- Settled but unpaid earnings are released subject to risk holds
- KYC records are retained for 7 years per Income Tax + GST
- This Agreement's surviving clauses (confidentiality, indemnity, dispute
  resolution, IP) survive

## 9. TDS and GST

### 9.1 TDS u/s 194O

The Income Tax Act, 1961, Section 194O, requires e-commerce operators to
deduct tax at source at 1% (or such other rate as the Government may
notify) on the gross amount of sales of services through their platform.
SaaradhiGo will deduct 1% TDS on the gross fare of each trip and remit it
to the Central Government against Your PAN. You can claim credit for this
TDS in Your income tax return.

### 9.2 GST

If Your aggregate turnover (across all sources) crosses the GST registration
threshold prescribed under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017
(currently INR 20 lakh for services), or if You are otherwise required to
register, You must obtain GST registration and provide Your GSTIN to
SaaradhiGo. SaaradhiGo's invoicing and credit treatment will then change
accordingly.

### 9.3 Income tax filing

You are responsible for filing Your own income tax return covering the
income earned through the Platform.
