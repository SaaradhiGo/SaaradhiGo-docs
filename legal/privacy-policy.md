## 1. About this Policy

1.1 This Privacy Policy describes how {{COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME}} ("**SaaradhiGo**",
"**We**", "**Our**") collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects personal
data about You when You use the SaaradhiGo mobile application, the VahanGo
mobile application, the SaaradhiGo website, and related services
(collectively, the "**Platform**").

1.2 SaaradhiGo is the **Data Fiduciary** under DPDP for the personal data You
share with the Platform. Where We engage third parties to process data on Our
behalf (payment gateways, mapping providers, notification providers, etc.),
those parties act as **Data Processors** under contractual data-processing
agreements with Us.

1.3 By using the Platform You consent to the processing of Your personal data
as described in this Policy. Where We rely on consent as the legal basis for
a particular processing activity, You may withdraw that consent at any time
(see Section 9).

## 3. How We use Your data

### 3.1 To provide the service

- Match riders with driver partners
- Compute fares (server-side, using server-validated distance)
- Process payments and refunds
- Send ride status updates (push, SMS, in-app)
- Show trip history and receipts
- Apply ratings and reviews

### 3.2 For safety and compliance

- Verify driver eligibility (licence, RC, insurance, permit, etc.) under MVA 2020
- Run SOS / panic flows: alert ops, alert Your emergency contact, alert emergency services where applicable
- Detect and prevent fraud (fare manipulation, abusive cancellations, account takeover)
- Respond to lawful requests from law enforcement (Section 91 CrPC, Section 67-69 IT Act, etc.)

### 3.3 For operations

- Operate, secure, and improve the Platform
- Conduct internal analytics (in aggregate, where possible without identifying You)
- Service reliability and capacity planning
- Customer support

### 3.4 For marketing (with consent)

- Send service announcements (operational, opt-out via in-app settings)
- Send marketing communications about new features, offers, or partners (opt-in only; opt-out via in-app settings or unsubscribe link in each email)

### 3.5 What We will NOT do

- We will not sell Your personal data
- We will not use Your data to make solely-automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on You without human review (DPDP requirement)
- We will not share Your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes
- We will not access Your microphone, camera, photos, or contacts except where You explicitly initiate a function that requires it (e.g. uploading a profile photo, in-app SOS audio recording if implemented)

## 7. Security

7.1 We implement reasonable security practices as required under Section 43A
of the IT Act and the Reasonable Security Practices Rules, including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+ for all client-server communication)
- Encryption at rest for sensitive identifiers (driver KYC documents in
  encrypted S3 storage)
- Access control on the principle of least privilege; admin actions audit-
  logged
- Secrets stored outside source code, in environment-restricted secret
  stores
- Password-less authentication for users (OTP-based) with short-lived JWT
  tokens, rotation, and revocation on logout
- Periodic security review and dependency scanning
- An incident response process for breach detection and notification

7.2 No system is 100% secure. If We learn of a personal data breach that
affects You, We will notify You and the Data Protection Board within the
timeframes required by DPDP and applicable law.

## 9. Your rights under DPDP

You have the following rights with respect to Your personal data:

### 9.1 Right of access — `GET /api/v1/auth/me/export/`

You can request a copy of all personal data We hold about You via the in-
app "Download my data" option or by emailing {{DPO_EMAIL}}. The export is
delivered within 30 days.

### 9.2 Right to correction

You can update Your profile data in-app. For fields that are read-only
post-signup (such as Your mobile number, which is Your identity for OTP
login), email {{DPO_EMAIL}} with proof of ownership of the new number.

### 9.3 Right to erasure — `POST /api/v1/auth/me/delete/`

You can delete Your account in-app at any time. On deletion:

- Identity fields (name, email, date of birth, address, emergency contact,
  profile photo, push tokens) are anonymised
- Your phone number is replaced with a placeholder so foreign-key
  references in trip and payment records continue to work for legal
  retention
- The account is deactivated; sessions are revoked
- Financial records (trips, payments, transaction history) are retained
  for 7 years as required by Indian tax law, but cannot be linked back to
  Your identity through anonymisation
- Marketing communications stop within 72 hours

### 9.4 Right to nominate a representative

You may nominate any other individual to exercise Your rights in the event
of Your death or incapacity. To nominate, write to {{DPO_EMAIL}}.

### 9.5 Right to grievance redressal

If You have a grievance about how We process Your personal data, contact
the Data Protection Officer at {{DPO_EMAIL}}. We will acknowledge within
72 hours and resolve within 30 days. If You are not satisfied, You may
file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India.

### 9.6 Right to withdraw consent

Where We process Your data based on consent (marketing, optional features),
You may withdraw consent at any time via in-app settings or by emailing
{{DPO_EMAIL}}. Withdrawal does not affect processing already undertaken
based on prior consent.

### 9.7 Right to information about processing

This Privacy Policy serves as the standing notice of how We process Your
data. For specific, ad-hoc disclosures (e.g. a particular law-enforcement
request), We will inform You unless prohibited by law from doing so.
