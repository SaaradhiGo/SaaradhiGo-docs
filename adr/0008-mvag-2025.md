# ADR-0008: MVAG 2025 — aggregator rules as config per country and state

* Status: Accepted
* Date: 2026-11-09
* Deciders: Founder (brief of 9 Nov), Architect
* Supersedes: the 1.5× hard rule in ADR-0002 (the mechanism stays)
* Superseded by: none

## Context

The Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines 2025 replace the 2020 guidelines the code followed.
They give central defaults, and each state adopts them with its own values. The rules are:

* a dynamic fare between 0.5× and 2× of the base fare, with the base fare notified by the state;
* the fare calculation disclosed to the rider;
* an own-vehicle driver keeps at least 80% of the fare;
* an unjustified cancellation costs 10% of the fare, at most ₹100, for rider or driver;
* the driver holds health insurance (₹5,00,000), term insurance (₹10,00,000) and 40 hours of
  induction training.

## Decision

Every value is config per market, never a literal.

1. **`pricing.MarketRegulation`** has a country row and an optional state row. It holds the
   fare band, the base-fare source, the cancellation fee (percent, cap, free window) and the
   driver-records gate (on/off, the two sums insured, the training hours).
   * A NULL state field uses the country value.
   * A change is a `pricing.change` maker-checker request and is audited.
2. **Fare quote** (`pricing.services.quote_fare`). The cap is the lowest of the rate card, the
   state and the country. The floor is the highest of the state and the country. The 0.90×
   over-supply discount respects the floor.
   * Hyderabad keeps its stricter 1.5× card cap, and the Telangana row stays unset until the
     state notification is confirmed.
   * The rider reads "Higher demand" or "Lower demand", never "surge", on the quote, the
     estimate and the receipt.
3. **Commission.** India's `CommissionCountryConfig.max_percent` is 20%.
   * A `CommissionStateCap` can only be lower than the country cap.
   * An active rule above a cap is never changed by the system. It raises a critical Finance
     alert (`pricing.commission_above_cap`) and is listed by the config API.
4. **Cancellation** (`ride.cancellation_penalty`, flag `cancellation_penalty`, on for India).
   * Rider: the fee is due on the next trip. It is shown before booking, added to that trip's
     payable amount, and paid to the driver who was let down as a `cancellation_compensation`
     ledger credit. On a cash trip the collecting driver has a matching
     `cancellation_fee_collected` debit.
   * Driver: a `cancellation_penalty` ledger debit. The 3-in-24h lockout stays.
   * No fee is charged for safety, for a no-show by the other side, or inside the free window.
5. **Driver records** (`driver.records`). Insurance and training fields are on `Driver`. The
   AIS-140 and panic-button fields are on `Vehicle`, with no gate.
   * When the gate is on, the daily expiry sweeper and the accept path block a driver who
     does not meet it. The gate is off by default.

## Consequences

* A state that notifies new values is a console change with a checker, not a release.
* Each rule's before/after fare is pinned by tests on fixed example trips
  (`tests/test_mvag_2025_pricing.py`).
* The rider's payable amount can now exceed the fare by a due cancellation fee, which is shown
  before booking and as its own receipt line.
